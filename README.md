### Hi, I'm Aspen Ham

**EAST facilitator who designs AI-assisted classroom tools and helps other teachers build theirs.**

I build with AI app builders (Base44) and AI-assisted development. My focus is the instructional design: what the AI should and shouldn't do, how it's grounded in real student work and teacher rubrics, and where the teacher stays in the loop.

- **[EAST Dashboard](https://github.com/hamaeast/east-classroom-ai-portfolio)** · [live demo](https://hamaeast.github.io/east-classroom-ai-portfolio/)
  A project-based classroom app used daily by 100 students. AI feedback on goals and evidence-backed check-ins, rubric scoring, and standards-based learning evidence, all with teacher review, documented prompt design, and a security review.
